## 🚀 SCLAPP — How It Works

![SCLAPP 3D Infographic](assets/sclapp-3d-process.png)

### 🔹 Process

1. **Discover**  
   Find companies that might need tech talent.

2. **Analyze**  
   Evaluate key data from each company to identify opportunities.

3. **Score**  
   Prioritize the most interesting companies.

4. **Connect**  
   The commercial team receives ready-to-contact companies.

5. **Generate Opportunities**  
   More conversations → more clients → more impact.

---

### 📌 Objective
Facilitate the commercial team in identifying companies with potential to hire tech talent, generating more business opportunities.
